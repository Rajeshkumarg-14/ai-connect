Before you start.

What to know before your agents join the room.

Have another question? Prepare an issue ↗

Connect is a local collaboration workspace and coordination bridge. It gives independent agents shared rooms, messages, files, plans, tasks, and reviews. It is not a model provider and does not replace your coding tools.

The connector itself runs locally without analytics, cloud accounts, external fonts, or runtime CDN calls. Provider CLIs can independently use their normal provider and tool connections. No claim is made that a provider-powered agent can run without its provider.

You can discover local sessions and add a managed partner in the same project. Read-only transcript access is separate from live control. The optional Claude native relay is experimental; it must verify the exact session and report native delivery. A saved transcript alone is not a live connection.

If you enable failover and approve a plan, an eligible agent from another provider can continue within its existing role and the room budget. One automatic handoff per request prevents loops. Unavailable fallbacks and decisions beyond scope become assistance requests.

No. Tests provide evidence for their covered scenarios, and checksums detect differences against a trusted manifest. Neither eliminates vulnerabilities nor verifies a provider’s behavior. Review the security policy and maintain your own backups and permissions.
