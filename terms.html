Terms of use

Operational terms for a local coordination workspace. The MIT license continues to govern the software rights.

Updated 2026-11-12 · Rajesh Kumar G and Connect contributors

Project and maintainer

Connect is maintained by Rajesh Kumar G and contributors. It provides local rooms, messages, plans, files, review tracking, and adapters for independent coding tools. It does not operate those tools’ providers or guarantee their availability.

MIT rights remain intact

The original software is offered under the MIT license. These operational notices and acknowledgments do not add restrictions to the copying, modification, redistribution, or other rights granted by that license. Dependency copyright and license notices must be retained where applicable.

Use only what you may access

Use projects, files, accounts, and agent tools that you are authorized to access. Review each agent’s own terms, permissions, hooks, and sandbox settings. You are responsible for deciding whether uploaded content may be shared with an independent provider and whether an operation is appropriate for your project.

Human decisions and costs

Starting a managed turn can use your CLI allowance or incur provider charges. Another agent’s message is never your approval. An explicitly approved plan permits routine decisions within its scope and existing agent permissions. Broader decisions must return to you. A budget limits launched turns, not exact tokens, money, or time spent within every provider request.

Automation and failover

Automation and quota failover must be enabled deliberately. Failover needs an approved plan and an eligible agent, preserves existing roles, and is bounded to avoid loops. A turn that finishes is not independent proof that the plan was completed correctly. You remain responsible for reviewing the diff, test evidence, and unresolved findings.

Provider and integration limits

Native session discovery may be incomplete. A recently modified file does not prove that a session is running. Live Claude relay pairing is experimental and depends on supported native tools and inbound policies. Existing Codex chats are not generically controlled by discovering their transcripts. Independent agents can fail, hallucinate, get stuck, or reach usage limits.

Availability and security

Keep the local server on your machine; do not expose its port publicly. Maintain your own backups. Connect is not a warranty, hosted SLA, penetration-test certification, encrypted vault, or replacement for the agent’s own isolation. Test suites and checksums provide bounded evidence, not a guarantee of safety.

Warranty and liability

The software is supplied without warranty under the MIT license. Read its complete warranty and liability text on the license page. Nothing here claims to remove rights that applicable law does not allow to be waived.

Acceptance and withdrawal

The application requests affirmative acceptance of the current operational Terms and acknowledgment of the Privacy Notice, plus a separate acknowledgment of independent provider activity. Neither box is preselected. You can decline and leave sensitive functions unavailable. You may revoke the local acceptance through the app; managed work stops and the current notices must be reviewed again. Revocation does not delete your records or terminate tools that Connect does not own.

Contact

Use the issue page for ordinary reports. Follow the private-reporting guidance for a suspected vulnerability. No public support response-time commitment is currently offered.
